The problem

A customer says they have paid. The merchant's POS has no network and cannot reach the bank. Release the goods and risk a fake or reused receipt, or turn away a genuine customer.

1. AskThe merchant enters the amount. The app creates a payment request and shows a QR code. This works offline.
2. PayThe customer scans it and pays through a Zenith channel. Zenith signs a receipt for that exact request.
3. Verify offlineThe merchant scans the receipt. The app checks the signature, the request, the amount and any reuse, in milliseconds.
4. ReconcileWhen the merchant reconnects, the log syncs. Zenith confirms, recovers or flags every entry and scores the risk.

Why it is safe

  • Tally, not trust. Like a split tally stick, the merchant holds one half (its request) and Zenith's signature is the other. They must match.
  • Ed25519 signatures mean an altered amount or a forged receipt fails instantly.
  • Tamper-evident log. Every entry is chained to the one before it, so edits and deletions are caught.
  • Bounded risk. Each device has an offline allowance, so the bank's exposure has a ceiling.
  • Fraud scoring with plain-language reasons for bank staff to review.
